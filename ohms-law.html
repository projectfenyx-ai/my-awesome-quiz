Ohm's Law — 12 Formulas

Example values:

  • I = amps = 10 Amps
  • E = volts = 120 Volts
  • R = ohms = 12 Ohms
  • P = watts = 1200 Watts

Amps (I)

Amps = √(Watts / Ohms)
I = √PR = √120012 = √100 = 10 Amps
Amps = Watts / Volts
I = PE = 1200120 = 10 Amps
Amps = Volts / Ohms
I = ER = 12012 = 10 Amps

Watts (P)

Watts = Volts² / Ohms
P = E²R = 120²12 = 1440012 = 1200 Watts
Watts = Volts × Amps
P = E × I = 120 × 10 = 1200 Watts
Watts = Amps² × Ohms
P = I² × R = 10² × 12 = 1200 Watts

Volts (E)

Volts = √(Watts × Ohms)
E = √P × R = √1200 × 12 = √14400 = 120 Volts
Volts = Amps × Ohms
E = I × R = 10 × 12 = 120 Volts
Volts = Watts / Amps
E = PI = 120010 = 120 Volts

Ohms (R)

Ohms = Volts² / Watts
R = E²P = 120²1200 = 144001200 = 12 Ohms
Ohms = Watts / Amps²
R = PI² = 120010² = 12 Ohms
Ohms = Volts / Amps
R = EI = 12010 = 12 Ohms
